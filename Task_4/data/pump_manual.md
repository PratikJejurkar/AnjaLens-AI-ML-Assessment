# Hydraulic Pump Model X-200 Manual

## Section 1: Safety
Always wear safety goggles when operating the X-200. Ensure the area is dry.

## Section 2: Operation
To start the pump, turn the green valve clockwise. 
The maximum pressure is 3000 PSI. Do not exceed this limit.

## Section 3: Maintenance
Check oil levels weekly. If the oil is dark, replace it immediately.
